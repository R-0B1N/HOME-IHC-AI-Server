Step 5: Lead Status Classification
Lead Status
Description & Criteria
🔥 Hot Lead
Complete property info and supporting documents provided and recorded. Landlord agrees to market and is ready for tenant inquiries/viewings immediately.
🌤️ Warm Lead
Landlord is interested in renting out, but key details or supporting documents are still pending to fill into the form.
❄️ Cooling Lead
Initial interest shown, but non-responsive after follow-up requests for database info, or has postponed rental plans.
⛔ Unqualified
Property unavailable/rented out, owner decided not to proceed, or invalid inquiry.
