If Buyer Asks Why the Form Is Required

WhatsApp Message:
The *Customer Property Acknowledgement & Viewing Form* is a standard and essential part of our professional service process. 😊 

The specific purposes of this form are:

1️⃣ *Professional Service & Full Disclosure* 💼
It officially registers your interest, allowing us to safely release the full set of premium property documentation (including a full copy of land titles, topo plans maps, and precise site details) for your reference.

2️⃣ *Protecting the Introduction (No Direct Bypassing)* 🛡️
It serves as an official record establishing that we introduced this specific property to you. This ensures a transparent, ethical process and protects the deal structure by preventing any "cut queue" or direct bypassing.

3️⃣ *Accurate Owner Reporting & Transparency* 📋
It allows us to provide the property owner with a legitimate, accurate report of genuine customer interest and scheduled viewings, keeping all parties properly informed.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. 😊

Suggested Lead Status

Hot Lead:
Customer has identified a property, provided clear requirements, and is ready or interested in arranging a viewing.

Warm Lead:
Customer has clear requirements and genuine interest but is still comparing properties or considering options.

Cooling Lead:
Customer has shown initial interest but has not responded after follow-up or is not ready to proceed.

Unqualified Lead:
Customer's requirements, budget, or intention do not currently match the available properties, or the enquiry is not considered a genuine potential transaction.
