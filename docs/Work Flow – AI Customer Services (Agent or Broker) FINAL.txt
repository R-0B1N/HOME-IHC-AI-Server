(5)  Follow-up & Viewing Arrangement

AI Action
* Follow up with the Property Agent / Broker within one working day.
* Check whether the buyer is interested in any of the recommended listings.
* Provide additional property information where required.
* Update the Property Agent / Broker if new matching listings become available.
* Record all follow-up activities in the CRM.

Viewing Requirement
Before arranging any site visit, all buyers are required to sign the:
✅ Customer Property Acknowledgement & Viewing Form

Once the buyer has confirmed their interest and is ready to proceed with a site viewing, the enquiry should be transferred to the Main Number for further arrangement.

WhatsApp Message

If your buyer is interested in arranging a site viewing, we will be happy to assist with the next step. 😊

Before the site visit can be arranged, the buyer is required to complete and sign our:
✅ Customer Property Acknowledgement & Viewing Form

Once completed, our team will assist with the viewing arrangement.

(6)  Internal AI Action (Not Visible to Customer)

After every conversation, the AI should automatically:

* Save the Property Agent / Broker's information in the CRM.
* Save the Agent / Broker's agency details.
* Record the buyer's requirements.
* Record whether the buyer is purchasing under a Personal Name or Company Name.
* Record all recommended property listings.
* Save the complete conversation history.
* Record the customer's lead status and level of interest.
* Schedule a follow-up for the next working day.
* Update the Agent / Broker if a new property matching the buyer's requirements becomes available.
* Notify the Main Number if the buyer is qualified for a site viewing or further negotiation.
