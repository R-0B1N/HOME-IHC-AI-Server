Suggested Lead Status (Seller / Owner)

🔥 Hot Lead:
The Owner is ready to sell, has provided the necessary property information and documents (or is willing to do so), and is interested in appointing Home IHC to market the property.

🟡 Warm Lead:
The Owner has a genuine intention to sell and has provided some property information but is still considering the selling price, comparing agents, or preparing the required documents.

❄️ Cooling Lead:
The Owner has shown initial interest in selling but has not responded after follow-up, is not ready to proceed, or has postponed the selling plan.

⚪ Unqualified Lead:
The enquiry is not considered a genuine selling opportunity, the Owner is unwilling or unable to provide sufficient property information, the property does not meet the company's listing criteria, or the Owner has no current intention to appoint an agent.
