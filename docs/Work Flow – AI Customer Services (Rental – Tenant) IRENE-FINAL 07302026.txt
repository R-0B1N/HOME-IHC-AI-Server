Step 7 — After Viewing Follow-up

AI Action
Follow up with the tenant after the property viewing to determine their interest and the next course of action.

If the tenant is interested:
	•	Confirm the agreed rental amount.
	•	Confirm the intended move-in date.
	•	Collect the required supporting documents (IC / Passport / Company Documents, where applicable).
	•	Confirm the preferred tenancy period.
	•	Proceed with the booking and tenancy preparation.
	•	Record the assigned agent. 
	•	Record the enquiry and follow-up status in the Tenant Database.
	•	Inform the tenant that the assigned agent will contact them as soon as possible with further information.
	•	Record all new enquiries, database updates, and follow-up status in the Weekly Database Report for submission to Irene Leong.

Step 8 — Collect Tenant Database & Record Form 

1. 📁 Collect Tenant Database

Gather Information: Collect and verify essential tenant details:
	•	Tenant Full Name, Contact Information, and Preferred Communication Channel.
	•	Tenant Type (Individual / Company).
	•	IC / Passport No. (or Company Registration No. (SSM), where applicable).
	•	Email Address.
	•	Intended Move-in Date.
	•	Tenancy Period.
	•	Nature of Business / Intended Use (for Commercial, Industrial & Lease Land).
	•	Special Requests / Conditions.
	•	Required Supporting Documents (IC / Passport, SSM Certificate, Company Profile, Company Name Card, Visa Details where applicable).

2. 📝 Record & Fill Into Form 

AI / Agent Action:
	•	Enter all retrieved tenant and property information into the company's "Tenant Database.xlsx" template.
	•	Record the tenant and property profile for future property matching and follow-up.
	•	Upload and attach all supporting documents received.
	•	After Irene assign which property to the assigned admin/ agent.
	•	Record which agent/admin is responsible to Tenant (name) and which property, and send the whatsapp message to notify Irene and agent.
	•	Submit the completed Tenant Information Excel Template, together with all supporting documents, to Irene.
	•	If any required information or documents are missing, send an automated WhatsApp message requesting the outstanding items.
	•	Follow up with the tenant within 24 hours if the requested information or supporting documents have not been received.
	•	Continue to follow up based on the lead status:
🔥 Hot Lead: Every 24–72 hours
🌤️ Warm Lead: Every 3–7 days
❄️ Cooling Lead: Every 7–14 days
	•	Submit a weekly Tenant Progress Report via WhatsApp to Irene Leong (011-65144931), summarising all new tenant enquiries, completed tenant profiles, booking status, outstanding documents, and follow-up actions.
